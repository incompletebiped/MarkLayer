Redline by Simple Local Web

Mark it in red. We'll knock it out.

Circle a photo, point at a button, flag a typo, or tell us something just feels off. Every mark lands on our list, and you watch each one get checked off.

How it works

  1. Open your website in Redline. Your site shows up with a red pen on top, right in your browser. Nothing to install.
  2. Mark what you want changed. Circle it, point an arrow at it, or click to pin a note. Say it however you would say it to us in person.
  3. Watch it get done. Each mark goes on our to-do list. As we finish one, it gets checked off, and you can see that on the same page.
